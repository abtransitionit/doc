# Project Management — The How

- Who: **Project Manager**
- Plans and schedules the work.
- Manages the team and budget, monitors costs, and identifies savings.
- Resolves day-to-day issues.

# Project Governance — The Rules and Control

- Who: Project Sponsor or **Steering Committee**
- Decides who can approve major changes.
- Ensures compliance with company rules and policies.
- Oversees the project’s value and business justification.

## 2. Key Legend & Conventions

### Status Definitions

* 🟡 **`Draft`**: Work in progress; contents are subject to major structural changes.
* 🔵 **`In Review`**: Content complete; awaiting peer, technical, or stakeholder review.
* 🟢 **`Approved`**: Reviewed, signed off, and frozen as an active project baseline.
* 🔴 **`Archived` / `Superseded**`: Obsolete document maintained solely for historical audit trails.

### Project Phase (PP) Codes

* **`PP1` – Vision & Initiation:** High-level goals, scope, charter, and business validation.
* **`PP2` – Requirements & Analysis:** Functional/non-functional specs, user stories, and compliance.
* **`PP3` – Architecture & Design:** Technical blueprints, data models, API contracts, and UX specs.
* **`PP4` – Execution & Operationalization:** Test plans, deployment guides, user manuals, and runbooks.

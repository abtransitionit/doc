<h1 align="center">Roadmap</h1>



# Definition

## Roadmap
**What it is**
- A document about a project or product
- Used for **communication and alignment** with stakeholders, teams, and leadership.
- Answers the question: *"Where are we going with this project/product and when will we get there?"*

**What does it contain**
- Typically organized into **phases, milestones, or releases** with estimated timelines.
- Outlines the **vision, direction, and progress** of a project/product over time.
- Focuses on **high-level goals and deliverables**, not detailed task breakdowns.

# Definition

## Roadmap

- Is a **living document** that evolves as priorities, resources, and market conditions change.
- Usually presented as a **timeline** or **Gantt chart** showing key features and initiatives.
- May be categorized by:
  - **Time-based** (Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4)
  - **Theme-based** (Foundation, Growth, Optimization, Innovation)
  - **Status-based** (Planned, In Progress, Completed, On Hold)
- Helps **prioritize work** and manage stakeholder expectations.
- Does not replace a project plan; it provides the **strategic context** for it.

---

## Roadmap Log

- A **chronological record** of all changes made to the roadmap.
- Captures the **evolution** of the roadmap over time.
- Answers the question: *"What changed, why, and when?"*
- Serves as an **audit trail** for decision-making and accountability.
- Each entry typically includes:
  - **Date** of the change
  - **Author** who made the change
  - **Description** of what changed (e.g., milestone added, date shifted, feature removed)
  - **Rationale** for the change (e.g., "Priority shifted due to customer feedback," "Resource constraints")
- Helps **communicate** roadmap adjustments to stakeholders.
- Provides **historical context** for past decisions and pivots.
- Supports **retrospective analysis** (e.g., "Why did we delay this feature?")
- Can be maintained in:
  - A simple **Changelog** file in the documentation repository
  - A **spreadsheet** with timestamps
  - A **version-controlled** document (Git, SharePoint, Confluence)
- Is **distinct from** the roadmap itself: the roadmap shows *where we are going*, the log shows *how we got there*.

---

## 🔗 Relationship Between Roadmap and Roadmap Log

| Concept | Purpose | Focus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Roadmap** | Communicate the future direction | **Forward-looking** (what's next) |
| **Roadmap Log** | Record changes to the roadmap | **Backward-looking** (what changed) |

> 💡 The roadmap is the *plan*; the roadmap log is the *history of the plan*.

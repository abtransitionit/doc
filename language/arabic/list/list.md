**Notes:**
- **Tanwin** marks (Fathatan, Dammatan, Kasratan) are the double vowel marks used for indefinite nouns.
- **Shadda** indicates a doubled/geminated consonant.
- **Sukun** indicates the absence of a vowel (a consonant with no vowel sound).
- **Hamza** has multiple forms depending on the "seat" (carrier letter) it sits on.
- **Maddah** is a combination of Hamza and Alif used at the beginning of words.
- When you type a standard diacritic after a letter (e.g., ب + َ), the rendering engine automatically positions it correctly (above or below).

<h1 align="center">What is a Log</h1>

The concept of Log

# Definition

- A **chronological record** of changes made over time.
- Captures the **evolution** of an entity over time.
- Answers the question: *"What changed, why, and when?"*
- Serves as an **audit trail** for decision-making and accountability.
- Each entry typically includes:
  - **Date** of the change
  - **Agent** who made the change
  - **Description** of what changed (e.g., milestone added, date shifted, feature removed)
  - **Rationale** for the change (e.g., "Priority shifted due to customer feedback," "Resource constraints")

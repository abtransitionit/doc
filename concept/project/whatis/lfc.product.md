<h1 align="center">Product Lifecycle</h1>


- The sequence of [phases][phase whatis] that a product/service passes through from its first **Release** to its **retirement**.


Example:

```
Product/Service
   │
   └── Lifecycle
          │
          ├── Phase 0 — Release
          ├── Phase 1 — Operation (use, maintenance)
          ├── Phase 2 — Evolution
          └── Phase 4 — Retirement
```  

- The product starts its lifecycle after the projecst ends.

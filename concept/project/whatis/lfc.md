<h1 align="center">Project Lifecycle</h1>

# Definition
- The sequence of [phases][phase whatis] that a [project][home] passes through from its **initiation** to its **closure**.
- Every project follows a lifecycle, regardless of its [methodology][methodology list] (Waterfall, Agile, etc.).



# Project Lifecycle vs. Product Lifecycle
- A project lifecycle is about the project **itself** (temporary).
- A product lifecycle is about the product being created by a project (from conception to retirement).

**Example**
- The `MetaDoc` project to create the software can have a lifecycle of several monts
- The `MetaDoc` complete lifecycle from production and maintenance through to decommissioning, can be more than 10 years.
